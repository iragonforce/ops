Git replaces future source-revision backup copies. Existing mixed archives are retained because source equivalence alone does not prove their full recovery purpose is obsolete. No encrypted application backup destination is invented by this project; large/sensitive backup redesign requires a demonstrated application-specific requirement.

Size/recovery evidence: archived Qubit/Tensor/Nexus exports are 396,974,080 / 678,932,480 / 39,710,720 bytes. Their tar member catalogs were readable during selective-history preparation. `hermes.tar` and `nexus-hermes.tar` have equal SHA-256 content and the same inode (30,730,240 bytes): they are hard-link names, not two storage copies. Both names remain because removing one saves no data space and can break provenance references. Prior audit measured the single-profile and agent-tools recovery directories at approximately 1.17 GB and 1.15 GB; these mixed runtime archives are not ordinary source revisions. Recovery procedures are documented; no destructive full-restore exercise was performed against running services.
