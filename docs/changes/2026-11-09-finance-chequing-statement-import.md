## Limits and rollback

This finishes importing the **available Chequing statement group**, not proving there are no missing monthly statements, independently confirming the real individual account identifier, linking inter-account transfer counterpart activities, or validating a budget. Some intermediate running-balance transitions are still unresolved; distinguish the verified final cash equality from transaction-by-transaction reconciliation. `TRANSFER_IN`/`TRANSFER_OUT` were left unlinked rather than guessed; do not count them as income/expenses or silently net them against TFSA/CIBC without reviewing counterpart statements.

For targeted rollback, read the protected journal's exact saved activity IDs and source references; identify later dependent links/edits first, then delete only reviewed activity IDs via the app's supported path and verify count/cash changes. Forced batches are **not idempotent**; do not rerun their original commit payload even if an immediate valuation read lags. Restoring a prior database snapshot wholesale would erase later TFSA/Chequing/owner changes. Keep the protected snapshots/journals until retention and tested recovery are established; no recurring backup was installed in this step. One-shot scratch scripts can be removed after this stage.
