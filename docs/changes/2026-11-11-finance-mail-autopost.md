# Future-only CIBC credit notification posting

Date: 2026-11-11 (America/Toronto). Objective: add owner-requested near-real-time purchase-email→Wealthfolio posting for the **one verified CIBC credit-card template** without guessing other cards, duplicating existing bank activity, or treating old notifications as new purchases. The existing iPhone→Receipt Wrangler→analytics and hourly candidate-only receipt↔activity matcher remain intact.

## Evidence and route

Read-only comparison of **525 authenticated CIBC purchase-alert evidence rows** against all three live CIBC Wealthfolio accounts (5,035 / 3,209 / 426 activities) found **391 unique strong amount/CAD/date±3-day/merchant matches**, all in the CIBC **credit-named** account; **17 ambiguous** cases were likewise entirely inside that account; **117** had no strong match. A separate authenticated Proton Bridge scan found **one card label and one suffix across all 525 parsed purchase notifications**. None of the other two CIBC accounts matched. Account metadata does **not** expose the card's four digits, so this is a strongly evidenced **inferred** mapping, not independently confirmed account-number identity. Other CIBC subject categories (including debit-card notices and alternative purchase subjects) have not passed purchase-template/route validation; they are not auto-posted. The native importer successfully previewed one recent alert with no same-day exact-amount activity as a valid, non-duplicate CIBC credit withdrawal, without committing it.

A root-owned 0600 route at `/etc/nexus/secrets/finance-mail-route.json` records the exact label, suffix, Wealthfolio account ID/name hash, evidence count, **CAD $5,000** per-alert safety cap, and the UTC activation instant **2026-11-11T18:11:33.820784+00:00**. The suffix, account ID, merchant and source-key values are not in Git. Messages predating activation are deliberately not backfilled. The CIBC notification date is used as a provisional purchase date and is **not proof of bank settlement**; reconcile against later bank statements rather than importing that row twice.

## Changes and checks

- Source `hosts/nexus/files/opt/data/bin/finance-mail-post`, manual `man/nexus/finance-mail-post.1`, and four synthetic tests in `tests/finance/test_mail_post.py` were committed in **`436f0f1`**. The script requires the established CIBC exact sender/subject/body plus trusted Proton authentication-results with aligned CIBC DKIM, matching staged `transaction_email_alerts`, one protected card suffix/label and unchanged account. It scans the target account for an exact existing spending match before posting; ambiguity, unsupported card, excessive amount or an uncertain previous commit fail closed. A new notification is posted as a provisional `WITHDRAWAL` with a source-key marker only after a mode-0600 fsynced pre-commit journal is written to `/var/lib/finance-mail-post` (root 0700). Native saved-ID readback verifies the actual activity; a crash after potential commit leaves a journal for manual reconciliation and is **never automatically retried**. Each write uses a temporary least-privilege, two-hour PAT created only when needed and revoked with readback. No persistent scheduled write credential was introduced.

Newly posted activity is eligible for receipt matching on the **next** hourly run, not retroactively in the preceding match step. The existing timer remains enabled; no additional timer/service was added.

## Limits and rollback

Only future messages matching the single CIBC credit route are eligible. The one existing iPhone/Wrangler scan remains unmatched because no exact supported payment activity was found; no fake transaction or confirmed link was created. Other debit/credit institutions, unsupported CIBC templates, Crypto source valuation, the disputed repeated Wealthsimple credit-card fee and incomplete household budget remain separate blockers.

**Later read-only debit discovery:** Proton Bridge contains **31** authenticated CIBC `Point of sale purchase` debit-card notices, with one debit-card suffix and one account suffix; 27 are domestic-pattern and four say international. Their fixed HTML labels identify purchase location, purchase amount and a separate *threshold* amount. None of the 31 had a strong merchant-name match in Wealthfolio. All 31 had exact-amount/near-date candidates in the existing CIBC Chequing account; the 27 domestic notices had no such candidate in either other CIBC account, while three international notices also had coincidental candidates in the credit account. This is useful **inferred** account evidence, but not an independently confirmed debit account-number mapping or verified international currency/fee treatment. No debit notification was staged as a transaction or posted. An owner confirmation of the debit card's actual Wealthfolio account was requested but not received during this run; do not auto-route debit writes from amount/date coincidence alone.

To disable writes, remove the fourth `ExecStart` from the Git-managed unit, guarded-deploy that unit and run `systemctl daemon-reload`; leave the three evidence/matching steps enabled. For a **posted** journal, read the exact source marker, saved Wealthfolio ID and dependent candidate links; remove only a reviewed provisional activity through the native app, never a pre-existing transaction. A `precommit` or `committed_unverified` journal requires investigation before retry because the app commit may already have succeeded. Never restore the entire pre-write database over later owner data. The protected route and journals need inclusion in a future verified host-state recovery path; do not add plaintext suffix or credentials to Git.
