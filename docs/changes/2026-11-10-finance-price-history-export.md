# Protected purchase and observed price-history export

Date: 2026-11-10 (America/Toronto). Objective: give the owner a usable item-level table now, without representing incomplete bank coverage or unreviewed product aliases as a household budget. This is a read-only export of existing PostgreSQL analytics, not a second receipt or financial transaction importer.

The Qubit owner-only report is `~/.local/share/finance-infrastructure/reports/purchase-price-history-2026-11-10.csv`, with companion `purchase-price-history-2026-11-10.json`. The report directory is mode 0700 and both files mode 0600. CSV columns include purchase date, source, merchant, receipt/item reference, raw product name, quantity, line total CAD, observed line-total/quantity CAD, category/canonical-product placeholders and bank-match state. It joins **validated, non-duplicate** purchase receipts and receipt items to the existing candidate match table; it never promotes candidate bank links to confirmed transactions. No row-level personal or purchase detail was committed to Git or ordinary logs.

The extraction read 17 receipts and **74 item rows** (73 Metro email, one iPhone/Wrangler scan). Thirty-eight *item rows* belong to receipts with a candidate bank link; 36 item rows are unmatched, including the scanned receipt. These are item counts, **not** counts of distinct matched transactions. All 74 quantities were positive and line totals nonnegative. The write used exclusive creation, compared off-host SHA-256 and byte size, and an independent Qubit readback parsed 74 CSV rows, matched the companion checksum and rechecked private modes. Source PostgreSQL tables/views and Wealthfolio/Receipt Wrangler were not modified.

The report is a **point-in-time snapshot**, while `v_price_history` remains the live database view. Observed unit price divides the line total by the source quantity; different package sizes/units are not normalized, tax/discount allocation is not inferred, and canonical products/categories remain empty. Ten Metro receipt-to-bank links are candidate-only; the scanned receipt has no matching payment evidence. Do not sum this report as complete spending or treat a candidate link as confirmation. Next: review aliases, units, adjustments and unmatched receipts; complete account coverage and transfer reconciliation before a budget. To roll back this export, remove only the two Qubit report files after owner review; source tables and backup artifacts are unaffected. The containing ops Git revision records this read-only result.
