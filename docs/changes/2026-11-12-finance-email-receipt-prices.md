# Authenticated email → validated receipt → item-price tracking

Date: 2026-11-12 (America/Toronto). Objective: link authenticated Proton Bridge CIBC purchase notifications to actual validated Receipt Wrangler/Metro receipts, then expose each receipt's observed item price with separate email/bank evidence. Do not create a second bank expense or claim an unmatched purchase was paid.

## Evidence and change

Read-only comparison of the then-526 alert-evidence rows with the 17 validated, nonduplicate CAD analytics receipts found **16 unique one-to-one** amount/merchant/±3-day matches: all 16 Metro email receipts, and no match for the one iPhone/Wrangler scan. Their 73 line items were already validated and projected; the scanned receipt contributed one separately validated item. The old `finance-mail-ingest` looked only at Wrangler receipts, so it stored **zero** direct alert↔Metro links despite the matching evidence. A later scheduled four-step intake added one new authenticated alert and posted one corresponding provisional Wealthfolio credit purchase, bringing the live staged alert count to **527**. Neither newly posted credit purchase had a validated matching receipt at deployment.

Updated the existing Git-owned `hosts/nexus/files/opt/data/bin/finance-mail-ingest` rather than introducing a new service, timer or database. It now considers **only validated, nonduplicate CAD `purchase_receipts`** from all receipt sources. Each alert needs exact CAD cents, a date within three days, a strong normalized merchant and unique ownership both ways. A new nullable `transaction_email_alerts.purchase_receipt_id` references the validated receipt; a unique partial index prevents two alerts claiming one receipt. The existing `wrangler_receipt_id` remains populated only for an actual scanned Wrangler receipt, preserving its prior semantics. No raw mail body or card suffix is persisted. The dynamic `v_purchase_item_tracking` view returns one row per validated item: receipt/date/store, raw or reviewed canonical product, recorded quantity, line total, observed price per **recorded** quantity in CAD, and **separate candidate/unmatched email and bank states** plus bank activity/account/date when linked. All validated receipt items remain observable even when the payment has not matched; this is price evidence, not an assertion of bank spend. Taxes and discounts remain receipt-level, not invented per-item allocations, and package/unit normalization remains an explicit gate.

The original `v_price_history` remains unchanged. The hourly order remains receipt projection → authenticated mail staging/linking → candidate-only receipt-to-bank matching → future-only CIBC credit posting; an expense posted in the fourth step is eligible for receipt-to-bank matching in the **next** run. The direct alert↔receipt candidate does not itself create an expense.

For immediate owner inspection, a point-in-time CSV of all **74** rows was written at Nexus `/var/backups/homelab/finance/reports/purchase-item-tracking-2026-11-12.csv` (root-only 0700 directory/0600 CSV and checksum manifest). A byte-hash readback and CSV parse verified 74 distinct item observations, 73 email-candidate item rows, 38 separate bank-candidate item rows and one unmatched scanned item. The columns include purchase date, merchant, product/raw name, recorded quantity, CAD line total and observed per-recorded-quantity price, plus independent email/bank match states. The CSV is a **snapshot**; the Git-owned live database view automatically reflects later validated receipts. Do not mistake this owner-only report for a complete or confirmed household budget.

## Limitations and targeted rollback

The one already uploaded iPhone scan remains unmatched because no supporting authenticated purchase alert or exact bank spending activity was found; do not force an arbitrary link. Ten Metro bank links and all 16 new direct alert↔receipt links are **candidates**, not confirmed transactions. Item observations are not normalized across brands/package sizes or allocated tax; broad household budget and recurring backup/retention remain incomplete. The Crypto historical CSVs and the unresolved credit-card duplicate/share-lending events are separate import exceptions. Leave debit-card destination confirmation until after the core receipt/price path, as requested by the owner.

To roll back only this change, stop the hourly oneshot before replacing the script with its previous Git revision through guarded deployment; then review candidate consumers and clear only the 16 affected `purchase_receipt_id` values/match states. Drop the new view if no dependent queries exist; retaining the harmless nullable column/index is safer than dropping schema while later data may depend on it. Do **not** restore the entire production analytics database over later receipt/email arrivals or delete the independently posted Wealthfolio credit activity. Protected backup manifests and live source-key/receipt IDs remain outside Git for targeted recovery.
